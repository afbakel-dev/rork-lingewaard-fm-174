## Notes

- Point 2 is the important one — it links the developer account name to the station name.
  That mismatch is the usual trigger for 5.2.3 on radio apps.
- If you do not know your Buma/Stemra or Sena licence numbers, ask your station's
  administration, or simply write "licensed via Buma/Stemra and Sena" and attach the
  invoice or certificate instead.
- Keep it to one page. Signed scan as PDF, attach in the Resolution Center reply.
